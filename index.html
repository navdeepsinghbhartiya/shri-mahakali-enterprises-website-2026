About Shri Mahakali Enterprises

Shri Mahakali Enterprises is a local retail store serving customers in and around Lakhna, Bakewar and Etawah. From everyday general-store essentials to stationery, gifts and sports equipment, the store brings a variety of useful products together under one roof.

Find us close to the Surya Vatika main gate on Ganj Muhal Nala Road, open every day from morning until late evening.
